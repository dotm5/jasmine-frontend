A personal fork of [ComicSparks/jasmine](https://github.com/ComicSparks/jasmine),
focused on discovering, reading, and organizing comics.

## Features

- **Discover** — Browse categories, search for comics, and explore weekly selections.
- **Read** — Navigate chapters and adjust reading preferences.
- **Organize** — Keep favorites in folders and revisit your reading history.
- **Download** — Save comics for offline reading and manage local downloads.
- **Community** — Read and post comments.
- **Personalize** — Choose themes and customize the reading experience.
